Magic bytes: 50594D50 ("PYMP")
Metadata record stream (same framing as PYCD and PYSC):
  Each record begins with a ULEB128 length, including its one-byte record ID
  but excluding the length itself. The remaining length - 1 bytes are data.
  Record IDs:
    01 - Filename
    02 - Menu definitions
    Other IDs can be skipped using the record length.
  A zero-length record (00) terminates the stream. The .mpy payload follows.
  PYMP 00 followed by .mpy bytecode is an ordinary empty record stream.

Menu definitions (record ID 02)
  Length-delimited text; the record-stream terminator is not part of the menu.
  Consists of a list of directives, each ending with a newline
  Directives:
    #MENULABEL (text to show in modules menu)
    #MENUTITLE (title of first column in menu)
    #MENUTOP (title of next column in menu)
    #MENUITEM (text to display in menu)|(text to insert)
    #MENUITEM (text to display in menu)|(text to insert)|(cursor offset)
